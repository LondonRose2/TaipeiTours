Frequently Asked Questions

Are these tours private or will I be with strangers?
Every single tour is 100% private. It will just be you, your travel partners (up to 4 people total), and me. This allows us to move at your pace and customize everything on the fly!
Are food and transportation included in the price?
Yes! For the food tours, all tastings (enough for a very filling meal) and any MRT tickets used during the tour are included in the upfront price. There are no hidden fees.
I have strict dietary restrictions. Can you help?
Absolutely. Taipei is actually incredibly vegan and vegetarian friendly, but the options are often hidden behind untranslated menus. Just let me know your restrictions when booking, and I will tailor the food stops accordingly.
