Chapter 05 — Questions

Before you book.

Are the tours private?+
Yes. Every tour is just you, whoever you bring, and your guide — up to 6 guests per group.
What's included in the price?+
Your guide's time and route planning, plus whatever's marked as included on each tour (tastings, tea, transit, temple donations). Anything unmarked is paid as you go.
How much walking is involved?+
It varies — market strolls and the temple walk are easy pace, the Elephant Mountain hike is a real climb with stairs. Each tour listing notes the pace so you can pick what fits.
Can you build a custom route?+
Yes — send a message with what you're into and your available dates, and a route gets built around it.
